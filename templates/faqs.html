FAQs

How do I add an observation?

Open Local Trees, choose a tree, then use Add Observation to record wildlife, health alerts, notes, tags, and photos.

What counts as a health alert?

Use Health Alert for disease, visible damage, dryness, pests, broken limbs, or anything that may need attention.

How do points work?

Observations, photos, tree tags, and adoptions add points instantly and appear in your profile activity.

Where is the care guide?

The main PDF is linked above. Tree-specific care guide links also appear inside each tree detail About tab.
